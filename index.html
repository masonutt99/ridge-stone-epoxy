FAQ

Epoxy floor questions, answered

What is a lifetime bonding warranty?

Every floor we install comes with a lifetime bonding warranty. It's our promise that your epoxy floor is built to last: no peeling, no cracking, and no redoing your floor in a few years. We can stand behind it because we don't cut corners on prep and we don't use cheap materials. Ask us for the details when you get your free quote.

How long does an epoxy floor installation take?

It depends on the size of the space, the condition of the concrete, and the system you choose. We offer 1 day floor restoration for the right projects. We'll tell you whether your floor is a good fit and give you a timeline with your free quote.

What kinds of spaces do you install epoxy floors in?

Garages, basements, kitchens, living areas, shops, and commercial spaces. If you can dream it, we can do it. Tell us about your space and we'll recommend the right system.

What's the difference between flake and metallic epoxy floors?

A flake floor has decorative color flakes broadcast into the epoxy for a textured, speckled finish that hides dirt and wear well, which makes it a popular pick for garages, basements, and shops. A metallic epoxy floor uses metallic pigments to create swirling, marbled, high-gloss patterns, so every floor is one of a kind. It's a popular choice for kitchens, living areas, and showpiece spaces.
